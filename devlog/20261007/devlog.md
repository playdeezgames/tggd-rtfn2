Feretory of SPLORR!! now saves, and has stopped letting the dead walk through doors

Yer maze has been rebuilt, and it looks mostly the same.

I rewrote the whole game in Odin and compiled it to WebAssembly. It is the same twenty rooms, the same words and the same tax forms. The jam version was VB.NET, and now it is a small Odin program that runs in yer browser with a bit of JavaScript to put buttons on the page.

What's new:

- The game saves. Every move is written down in yer browser, so you can close the tab, come back, and land right where you were: in the middle of a room, in the middle of a form, or in the middle of an ad break with the clock still running. Abandon and it forgets you.
- Starving now hurts every step. The old version quietly fed you a little as it hurt you, so health only dropped every other move. It was being kind, and that was not the idea.
- The dead and the insane can only look around and use the menus. Before, they could still walk through doors, sign forms and eat lunch. That seemed like too much ambition.
- Tax forms and ink wells no longer pile up on the same tile, so a door will not turn up with paperwork on it.
- Dropping part of a stack no longer erases the message telling you that you dropped it.
- The room disappears from the screen when you abandon, instead of haunting the main menu.
- The name box starts with Olen Kyrpa, who was always supposed to be the default and was never used. Type over him. A name is required.
- Fixed "Compleness" and "a Ink Well". They are now correct, which will make them harder to love.

What's the same: the maze, the keys, the food, the pens, the poop, and the fact that there is no way to win. That is the point of the metaphor.

The sponsored ad breaks are still there too, still two minutes, and umlaut.fyi, Pen 15 and Jargonize are still very glad you are here.

One thing that is going away: the Windows, Linux and Mac console downloads. The browser version is the one I'll keep updated, so the old downloads are being retired.
