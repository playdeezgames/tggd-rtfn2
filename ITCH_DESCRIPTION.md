# Feretory of SPLORR!!

*A metaphor about forms. And poop.*

You wake up in a room. The room has doors. Beyond the doors are more rooms, twenty in all, and somewhere in them are food, pens, ink wells, keys and a great many tax forms.

Eat before you starve. Poop before it hurts. Keep yer sanity, which the forms will try to take. Some doors are locked, and the keys are in the other rooms. There is no way to win.

**HOW TO PLAY**

It is a turn-based game of menus. Click a button to do the thing on the button: N, E, S and W to walk, Look, Status, and the things on yer tile (Ground, Inventory, doors, forms, ink wells). The game moves when you move.

The room is drawn on the left. `@` is you, `f` is food, `k` is a key, `/` is a pen, `+` is a door, `T` is a tax form, `I` is an ink well, `~` is what you left behind. Hover over anything to find out what it is.

Your game is saved in your browser as you go, so you can close the tab and come back to where you were.
